Chapter 1: The Number's DNA — Digit Sums

The Secret Code: Navashesh ("Casting Out Nines")
The Superpower: Instantly checking if any mathematical answer is right or wrong!

Think of a Digit Sum as the "DNA" or the fingerprint of a number. You find it by simply adding all the digits of a number together until you are left with just one single digit (from 1 to 9).

The Shortcut: "Casting Out 9s"

Adding big numbers can slow you down. The fastest way to find a Digit Sum is to completely ignore the number 9, and ignore any numbers that add up to 9!

🧮 The Abacus Connection

Imagine you just finished a massive mental sprint. How do you know for sure you are 100% correct? You use the Digit Sum! The digit sum of your final answer must equal the digit sum of all the numbers you just added together.

Chapter 2: Speed Addition — Left to Right

The Secret Code: Puranapuranabhyam ("By Completion")
The Superpower: Adding numbers exactly the way you read them!

In school, you are taught to add from right to left: units first, then tens, then hundreds. In Vedic Maths, we add from Left to Right—the exact same direction you read a book.

🧮 The Abacus Connection

On the abacus, you naturally calculate from left to right. Remember your fingering rules: if you need to carry a 10 to the next rod, always use your left hand thumb finger for +10!
