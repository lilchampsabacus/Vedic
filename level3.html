Chapter 2: One-Line Polynomial Division

The Secret Code: Paravartya Yojayet (Advanced)
The Superpower: Dividing algebra as easily as normal numbers!

Example: (x³ + 3x² − 4x + 7) ÷ (x − 2)

Instead of long algebraic division, we use the Flag method from Level 2. The divisor is (x-2), so our Flag is +2 (flip the sign!).

  • Line up the coefficients: 1, 3, -4, 7.
  • Bring down the 1.
  • Multiply by Flag (+2) and add. 1 × 2 = 2. Add to 3 = 5.
  • Multiply by Flag (+2) and add. 5 × 2 = 10. Add to -4 = 6.
  • Multiply by Flag (+2) and add. 6 × 2 = 12. Add to 7 = 19.

Quotient: x² + 5x + 6. Remainder: 19.

Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry Triples

The Secret Code: Urdhva-Tiryagbhyam (Applied to Triples)
The Superpower: Doing Geometry without square roots!

Instead of using messy distance formulas, we use Pythagorean Triples (like 3,4,5 or 5,12,13). We can combine them using the exact same Crosswise multiplication pattern you learned in Level 1 to find compound angles and perpendicular distances instantly.

Chapter 9 & 10: Calculus Shortcuts

The Secret Code: Chalana-Kalanabhyam ("Differential Calculus")
The Superpower: Differentiating and Integrating in one breath.

Vedic Differentiation

For polynomials, simply multiply each coefficient by its power index, do a "1 down" on the power, and drop the constant.

Example: 3x⁴ − 2x³ + 5x² − 7x + 1

  • 4 × 3 = 12x³
  • 3 × -2 = -6x²
  • 2 × 5 = 10x
  • 1 × -7 = -7

Answer: 12x³ − 6x² + 10x − 7
